# Project CONTAGION
**Emerging Pathogen Research Institute (EPRI)**

Project CONTAGION is a simulation developed by the fictional Emerging Pathogen Research Institute (EPRI) 
to monitor the spread of a fictional pathogen, NV-26, across five U.S. cities over a 30-day period.

The simulation will use a map<string, array<list<string>, 3>> to organize individuals by city and health status. 
Each city will contain three lists representing healthy, infected, and recovered individuals. 
The program will read at least 100 individual records from an external file named population.txt.

Each day, healthy individuals will have a probability of becoming infected based on the city's infected population, 
while infected individuals will have a probability of recovering. Recovered individuals will remain immune for the remainder of the simulation.

The program will display the initial population statistics, track changes in each city's healthy, infected, 
and recovered populations over 30 days, and generate a final summary comparing the outbreak's progression across the five cities.
